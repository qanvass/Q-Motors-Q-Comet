## Rules

- Original fictional car only. Do not copy real vehicles or other games.
- Dimensions in `project.json` are provisional until Quasar approves a sheet.
- Do not fabricate `.yft`, `.ytd`, `.ybn`. Export only from reviewed Blender/Sollumz.
- Keep body, glass, lights, wheels, collision, and interior separable.
- Game cabin from `q comet cabin dash.png`. Cinematic dash is inspiration only.
- Four usable seats and clean first-person visibility.
- Never claim NoPixel affiliation, approval, or player install.
